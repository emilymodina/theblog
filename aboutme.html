About Me

Emily Modina

Insta: @emilymodina

Sup! I'm Emily, born and raised in San Diego, California. I've been an absolute fan of Garfield ever since I was little, thanks to my mom. It all began when I used to visit my mom's childhood home at least twice a month, and my grandma would save the weekly newspapers for me because I loved to read the comics featured at the end of each one. About 90% of the time, Garfield would be there taking up more than half the comic space.

Eventually, my mom gave me her old Garfield comic books to read. I still have them to this day! I then bought a bunch of the Garfield "Fat Cat 3-Pack" comic book series, and would store all of my books on a shelf with my first Garfield plush. I also enjoyed watching the old Garfield cartoons I'd find streaming on random channels. Now, making my own money, I've been getting into collecting vintage Garfield plushies!

I hope you enjoy the plushies - totally not because you can't actually buy them - as much as I do. Garfield's a pretty cool guy after all.
